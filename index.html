Your dentist

Meet Dr Nico Pretorius.

Dr. Nico has spent his career convincing nervous patients that the dentist isn't so bad after all. Ask anyone who's sat in his chair for a root canal, an extraction, or just a routine clean — they'll tell you the same thing: he explains everything before he does it, he doesn't rush you, and he always tells you exactly what to expect before you feel it.

He's also the one who'll squeeze you in on a fully booked day if you're genuinely struggling — and yes, that mug really is always on his desk.

  • Gentle with anxious & first-time patients
